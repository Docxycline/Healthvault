# MediVault

## Your medical history should follow YOU — not the hospital.

**Patient-owned. Consent-driven. Secure. Interoperable.**

MediVault is a patient-controlled digital health record platform designed to give people a secure way to store, manage, and selectively share their medical information with trusted healthcare professionals.

Instead of medical records being scattered across hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies, PDFs, and paper documents, MediVault brings them together into one controlled digital health vault.

The key difference is simple:

**The patient stays in control.**

---

## The Problem

Healthcare records are often fragmented across different hospitals, diagnostic centres, pharmacies, and personal documents.

This creates several problems:

- Medical history is scattered across multiple sources
- Patients repeatedly provide the same information
- Doctors may not have complete patient context
- Patients have limited visibility into who accessed their records
- Clinicians spend valuable time searching through documents
- Healthcare systems struggle to exchange information seamlessly

Healthcare has digitized medical records, but patients still lack a unified and transparent way to control how their information is shared.

---

## Our Solution

MediVault creates a patient-controlled medical record that allows users to:

### Collect

Bring medical information together from documents and healthcare sources.

### Structure

Use AI-powered document processing to extract useful medical information from unstructured records.

### Secure

Store health information inside a protected digital vault.

### Control

Let the patient decide who can access their information.

### Share

Give healthcare professionals access to specific information for a defined period.

### Audit

Maintain a transparent history of who accessed what and when.

---

## What Makes MediVault Different?

Traditional healthcare systems are often built around:

**Hospital → Patient Records**

MediVault moves toward:

**Patient → Controls → Healthcare Access**

The patient can decide:

- Who gets access
- What information is shared
- How long access lasts
- Whether access should be revoked

MediVault does not replace hospitals.

**It creates a patient-controlled layer across healthcare systems.**

## Patient Journey

Imagine visiting a new hospital.

### 1. Doctor Requests Access

The clinician requests the information required for the consultation.

### 2. Patient Receives a Consent Request

The patient can review:

- Requested data
- Requesting provider
- Access duration
- Purpose of access

### 3. Patient Grants Scoped Access

Instead of sharing the complete medical history, the patient can share only the required information.

### 4. Clinician Receives a Concise Summary

Relevant information such as:

- Allergies
- Current medications
- Diagnoses
- Recent medical records

can be presented in a focused clinician view.

### 5. Access Is Recorded

The patient can review the access event through the audit trail.
